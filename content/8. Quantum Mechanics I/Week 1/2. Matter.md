	- H-gamma -> 431.4 nm
	- H-beta -> 486.1 nm
	- H-alpha -> 656.3 nm

> [!Information]
> Hot hydrogen emits light in a set of spectral lines.
> Balmer series set of lines in the visible spectrum.

#### Bohr's Model of Hydrogen Atom
A small negatively charged electron orbits a small positively charged core (the proton) like a planet around a sun but with electrostatic attraction.

>[!Key Assumption]
>Angular momentum is "quantized" in units of Planck's constant, $h$, over $2\pi$. (Neils Bohr, 1913)
>$$\frac{h}{2\pi} \equiv \hbar$$

#### de Broglie Hypothesis
>[!de Broglie Hypothesis]
>A particle with mass also behaves as a wave with wavelength.
>$$\lambda = \frac{h}{p}$$
>$p \to$ Particle's Momentum
